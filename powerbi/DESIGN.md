# Orrin People: dashboard design

Orrin Digital is fictional, so this is a **proposed** identity for a UK people-analytics team, not an official brand.
People analytics reads best calm and light: indigo carries trust and seriousness, lavender softens it, and a single
warm accent (coral) is kept for risk so it stands out wherever it appears.

## Layout

- **Header**: white band with the logo (two overlapping circles, an original mark), pill tabs for the seven pages
  (indigo pill = current page) and the reporting date.
- **Filter rail** on the right: Year, Department and Location dropdowns, synced across pages, a one-click reset, and a
  three-line **"What this page says"** note so a reader gets the point before the charts.
- **Title row** with a coral marker and a live filter-context line.
- **KPI tiles**: white, with a status-coloured left edge (green / amber / red against people targets, or the
  meaning of the number) and a monthly trend line where the measure moves over time.
- Two content rows below. Tables fill their cards; bar charts that carry data labels drop the value axis.

This differs on purpose from the portfolio's other dashboards (05: dark sidebar; 06: charcoal top band).

## Files

- `Orrin.pbip`: open in Power BI Desktop (Nov 2025 or later) and Refresh.
- `theme/Orrin_People.json`: the theme on its own, importable into any report.
- Generated by [`scripts/build_pbip.py`](../scripts/build_pbip.py); do not edit the JSON by hand.
